The Property

Custom comfort, river out back

Welcome to 258 Rivers Edge Drive — a custom-built home from 2020, impeccably maintained, in the highly sought-after gated community of Rivers Edge Plantation. An open-concept floor plan fills with natural light, finished with luxury vinyl plank flooring, crown molding, newly added plantation shutters, and a gas fireplace anchoring the living room.

The dining area opens to the home's signature space: a back deck overlooking the Waccamaw River roughly thirty feet below — morning coffee, afternoon lemonade, and a view most listings can only borrow for the photos.

The main level offers the primary suite plus a guest bedroom with private full bath; upstairs, two more bedrooms, a full bath, and a large bonus room with closet that serves as the fifth bedroom. A two-car garage completes the home.
