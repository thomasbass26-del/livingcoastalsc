The Community

Charleston charm on the Intracoastal Waterway

Waterway Palms Plantation sits directly on a scenic stretch of the Intracoastal Waterway in Carolina Forest, between The Bluffs on the Waterway and Carolina Waterway Plantation. Behind the gates, custom homes blend Carolina Low-Country and Charleston-Row architecture — many three stories tall, with rear courtyards, balconies, and wrought-iron fencing, and gazebos scattered throughout the neighborhood.

The amenity village is the showpiece: the largest residential swimming pool in South Carolina — Junior Olympic lap lanes, swim-up refreshment center, jetted spa, and a landscaped island — beside a clubhouse with catering kitchen, a 24-hour fitness center, and courts for tennis, pickleball, basketball, and sand volleyball.

And the waterway is the point: a private boat ramp puts residents directly on the Intracoastal, with day docks for tying up and a gated storage area for boats, trailers, and RVs. Weekends here are measured in hours on the water.

Good to Know

Waterway Palms questions, answered

Is Waterway Palms Plantation a gated community?

Yes. Waterway Palms Plantation is a gated community located directly on the Intracoastal Waterway in the Carolina Forest area of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.

Does Waterway Palms Plantation have boat access?

Yes. Residents have a private boat ramp with direct access to the Intracoastal Waterway, day docks, and a gated storage area for boats, trailers, and RVs.

What amenities does Waterway Palms Plantation offer?

A two-story resort clubhouse with full kitchen, fitness center, and game room, a resort-style pool and separate children's pool, tennis, pickleball, bocce, and basketball courts, a playground, walking trails, and gazebos throughout the community.

What style are the homes in Waterway Palms Plantation?

Custom-built homes blending Carolina Low-Country and Charleston-Row architecture — many three stories, with rear courtyards, balconies, and wrought-iron detailing.

What schools serve Waterway Palms Plantation?

Waterway Palms Plantation is zoned for Ocean Bay Elementary School, Ocean Bay Middle School, and Carolina Forest High School.
