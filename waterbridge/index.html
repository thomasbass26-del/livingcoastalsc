The Community

Resort living in the heart of Carolina Forest

Behind Waterbridge's brick-and-wrought-iron gates, lantern-lit streets wind through coastal cottage-style custom homes set on wooded homesites and panoramic lakefronts. The community wraps more than 80 acres of lakes and is bordered by roughly 120 acres of federally protected woodland — a third of the neighborhood will never be developed.

The amenity village is the showpiece: the largest residential swimming pool in South Carolina — Junior Olympic lap lanes, swim-up refreshment center, jetted spa, and a landscaped island — beside a clubhouse with catering kitchen, a 24-hour fitness center, and courts for tennis, pickleball, basketball, and sand volleyball.

And then there's Palmetto Lake: sixty acres of open water in the middle of it all, with a community boat launch and storage — kayaks, paddleboards, and slow evening cruises just beyond your backyard.

Good to Know

Waterbridge questions, answered

What amenities does Waterbridge have?

Waterbridge features the largest residential swimming pool in South Carolina — with Junior Olympic lap lanes, a swim-up refreshment center, and a jetted spa — plus a village-style clubhouse, 24-hour fitness center, and courts for tennis, pickleball, basketball, sand volleyball, and bocce.

Is Waterbridge a gated community?

Yes. Waterbridge has two gated entrances framed in brick and wrought iron, off Carolina Forest Boulevard in Myrtle Beach.

Can you boat in Waterbridge?

Yes. Palmetto Lake is a 60-acre lake with a community boat launch and boat storage. Residents kayak, paddleboard, canoe, and fish; the community holds more than 80 acres of lakes in total.

What schools serve Waterbridge?

Waterbridge is zoned for Ocean Bay Elementary School, Ocean Bay Middle School, and Carolina Forest High School.

How far is Waterbridge from the beach?

About 15 minutes (roughly 7 miles), with quick access to Highways 501, 31, and 17. Tanger Outlets and Grand Strand Medical Center are about 10 minutes away.
